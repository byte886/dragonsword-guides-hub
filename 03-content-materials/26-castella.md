## 二、核心内容

### 2.1 Character Profile

- **Name**: Castella (卡斯特拉)
- **Faction**: Johnny Mercenaries (乔尼佣兵团) — NOT Red Fox (corrected from wiki error)
- **Role**: Member (团员)
- **Birthday**: July 29
- **Join**: Day 1 — automatically unlocked during Chapter 1, free
- **Weapon**: Heavy two-handed battle axe
- **Hero Trait**: Breaker (Crushing)
- **Status Effects**: Stun (primary), Airborne (Rising Heavy Strike), Knockdown (after Awakening 1)
- **Tier**: A (games.gg, Destructoid, UP Station); S (allthings.how team guide, insertcoins.press)
- **Role**: Control / Super Armor Break DPS
- **Description**: Golden long hair in high ponytail, pointed elf ears, light green eyes. Silver-white light armor with heavy metal shoulder guards, giant battle axe on back. Tall and athletic, wild and heroic. An eccentric elf fascinated by archaeology.

### 2.2 Playstyle

Castella is one of the first characters you unlock and remains effective into endgame. Her kit focuses on:
- **Breaking super armor** — Breaker trait with heavy axe hits
- **Crowd control** — Stun, Airborne, and Knockdown
- **Group gathering** — Whirlwind pulls enemies together
- **Ground control** — Wild Roar pushes enemies away

No complex mechanics to master — simple, dependable, and satisfying. Big weapon, huge swings. Whirlwind gathers enemies, uppercut launches entire groups into the air for Signal Skill follow-ups.

## 三、技巧与建议

1. **Whirlwind to group, then launch** — use Whirlwind to gather enemies, then follow with Rising Heavy Strike on knocked-down targets
2. **Break armor first** — Stun Strike only works on enemies with broken super armor; focus heavy hits to break before using it
3. **Awakening 1 is key** — adds Knockdown to Stun Strike, giving you two control effects in one skill
4. **Awakening 3 reduces enemy DEF** — Wild Roar's DEF debuff significantly increases team damage
5. **Free and early** — Castella unlocks automatically in Chapter 1, making her a core early-game investment
6. **Great for beginners** — no complex mechanics, straightforward crowd control
7. **Leaf of Vigor** — needed for Castella's second Breakthrough; farm Rusted Tusk Bandits' Treasure Vault (Very Hard: 10 per run)

## 四、常见错误

1. **Using Stun Strike on unbroken armor** — it requires broken super armor; you must break first
2. **Using Rising Heavy Strike without Knockdown** — it requires a knocked-down target
3. **Building her as pure DPS** — Castella's value is control and armor breaking; build ATK/Crit but position her as a control character
4. **Ignoring Whirlwind's mobility** — you can move slowly during Whirlwind to reposition while dealing damage

## 五、待确认/有争议

- **Faction discrepancy**: dragon-sword-awakening.wiki lists Castella as Red Fox Mercenaries, but GladiatorBoost and 游侠网 both list Johnny Mercenaries. Using Johnny Mercenaries as confirmed.
- **Tier ranking**: Most sources say A-tier, but allthings.how team guide and insertcoins.press rank her S-tier. Her team value (control + armor break) may exceed her individual DPS tier.
- **2-piece set**: English sources recommend Azure Gale; Chinese source recommends "Castle Destroyer" (+15% break damage). Both are viable depending on focus.
