## 二、核心内容

### 2.1 Character Profile

- **Name**: Roxy (罗克茜)
- **Faction**: Red Fox Mercenaries (红狐佣兵团) — Leader (团长)
- **Birthday**: April 10
- **Weapon**: Bow
- **Status Ailment**: Bleed (流血)
- **Tier**: A (consensus across games.gg, Destructoid, UP Station, 2upskill)
- **Role**: Ranged DPS / Bleed applier
- **Description**: Bright wine-red long straight hair with geometric headband, purple eyes, confident and capable demeanor. Navy blue cropped battle outfit with fluffy white shoulder decorations.

### 2.2 Playstyle

Roxy is the most approachable Bleed character in the roster. Almost every skill applies Bleed, making her consistent against high-health enemies and bosses. She excels in long encounters where sustained Bleed damage adds up.

Key traits:
- Ranged bow attacks keep her at safe distance
- Crimson Spiral is a forward dash that applies Bleed + Stun
- Bloody Shower fires rapid arrow volleys that Bleed all enemies in a line
- Kick Storm and Bleeding Grab manipulate enemy positioning
- Not the highest Bleed ceiling (Theresia is stronger), but easiest to use
- Pairs with Theresia (Stun enabler) for the strongest Bleed/Stun team

## 三、技巧与建议

1. **Crimson Spiral is your opener** — dash in applying Bleed + Stun, then follow with Kick Storm on the stunned target
2. **Bloody Shower for groups** — fires in a line, Bleeds every enemy it touches
3. **Stay at range** — bow attacks keep Roxy safe; use Slide Shot to reposition
4. **Stack Bleed to 2+** — Blood Blade Strike applies Suppression at 2+ stacks, which is a stronger control
5. **Mount giant bosses** — at 2+ Bleed stacks, chain attacks while riding for big damage
6. **Awakening 5 is a huge spike** — +15% damage to bleeding enemies + 15% DEF ignore
7. **Pair with Theresia** — the Bleed/Stun combo is currently the strongest team in the game

---

## 四、常见错误

1. **Using Kick Storm without Stun** — it requires a stunned target; without Theresia or another Stun source, this skill is unavailable
2. **Building pure ATK without Crit** — Roxy's Awakening 3 and 5 reward Crit; aim for 80% Crit Rate
3. **Playing her like a burst DPS** — Roxy is sustained damage through Bleed, not burst; long fights are where she shines
4. **Ignoring Air Dodge** — the bounce after diving provides both damage and repositioning; Awakening 3 adds Crit DMG stacks
