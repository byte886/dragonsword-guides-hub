## 二、核心内容

### 2.1 What are Familiars?

Familiars are traversal companions (mounts) in DragonSword: Awakening. The system unlocks shortly after joining Johnny's Mercenaries early in Chapter 1. They let players move faster through the open world of Orbis with special movement abilities.

Key facts:
- **29 familiars total** (All Things How + PGG agree; wiki says 30 — see disputed section)
- **8 types**: Insect, Squirrel, Cat, Bird, Sky Wolf, Direwolf, Gryphon, Dragon
- Familiars have **no combat stats** — value is purely traversal and type passives
- Most are claimed from the **Familiar Codex** after meeting a requirement
- 18 are free through gameplay, 10 are DLC-exclusive, 1 is Deluxe Pack-exclusive
- Abyssal Direwolf was a **pre-order bonus**, free until August 31, 2026

### 2.2 How Unlocking Works

1. Meet the requirement (story progress, Mercenary rank, activity count, currency, etc.)
2. Open the Familiar Codex
3. Claim the familiar
4. Equip it to your party

Some familiars unlock automatically through story progression. Others require grinding activities like mining, opening chests, or defeating world bosses.

### 2.3 Traversal Skills by Type

Each type has a fixed traversal skill (same for all familiars of that type):

| Type | Traversal Skill | What it does |
|------|----------------|--------------|
| Insect | None | No special movement skill |
| Squirrel | Climbing Jump | Jump higher while climbing cliffs |
| Cat | Swim Dash | Dash quickly in water |
| Bird | Air Dash | Quick forward dash in mid-air (3s cooldown) |
| Sky Wolf | Underwater Dash | Dash underwater |
| Direwolf | Boost Jump | Jump farther during Ground Boost (0s cooldown) |
| Gryphon | Charged Jump | Charged high jump |
| Dragon | Air Free Dash | Unlimited free aerial movement (strongest) |

### 2.4 Type Passive System

When you own **2 or more familiars of the same type**, you unlock that type's passive: keeping one familiar of that type in your party grants its movement skill to **all** familiars in the party.

Example: Owning 2 Direwolves means any equipped familiar can use Boost Jump.

This is why collecting duplicates within a type is valuable — it lets you use your favorite mount's appearance while having access to multiple movement skills.

The Brave Baby Dragon (Dragon type) does **not** follow this rule and does not provide a type passive.

Type passive bonuses (from 九游 screenshots):
- 2 of type: Skill available
- All familiars can use the skill
- Reduces stamina consumption by 20% during that movement type
- Increases movement speed by 10% during that movement type

### 2.5 Which to Unlock First

Priority familiars for early game (from PGG):
1. **Bright Crowtit** (Bird, Air Dash) — reach Novice Mercenary Corps rank; biggest exploration upgrade
2. **Refreshing Canary** (Bird, Air Dash) — complete 20 mini-games; second Bird for type passive
3. **Marple Flying Squirrel** (Squirrel, Climbing Jump) — reach Intermediate Mercenary Corps
4. **Stormy Direwolf** (Direwolf, Boost Jump) — reach Veteran Mercenary Corps
5. **Mist Wing Cat** (Cat, Swim Dash) — craft 100 equipment pieces

## 四、技巧与建议

1. **Prioritize Bright Crowtit first** — Air Dash is the biggest early exploration upgrade
2. **Get 2 of each type** for type passives — the second familiar of a type shares its skill with all mounts
3. **Abyssal Direwolf is free until August 31, 2026** — claim it from the DLC page even if you don't use it immediately
4. **Familiars have no combat function** — don't worry about "best" stats, pick based on movement needs
5. **Activity-based familiars come naturally** — mining, chest-opening, and puzzle-solving happen during normal play
6. **Brave Baby Dragon is the strongest** but requires Deluxe Pack — Air Free Dash has no stamina restriction
7. **DLC familiars are not stronger** — they provide the same skills as free familiars, just variety for type passives

## 五、常见错误

1. **Forgetting to claim from the Codex** — meeting the requirement doesn't auto-unlock; you must claim in the Familiar Codex
2. **Ignoring type passives** — collecting 2 of a type shares the skill across all familiars, which is a major upgrade
3. **Buying DLC familiars early** — free familiars cover all movement skills; DLC is for collectors
4. **Expecting combat benefits** — familiars are traversal-only, no combat stats or bonuses in battle
5. **Riding into combat** — you must dismount to fight; monster attacks can knock you off
