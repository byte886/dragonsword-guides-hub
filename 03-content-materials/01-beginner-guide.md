- 素材内容：

DragonSword: Awakening combines open-world exploration with a three-Hero tag-action combat system built around Status Ailments, Signal Skills and character switching. The game can initially feel more complicated than a standard action RPG because individual character strength is only one part of combat.

This beginner guide explains what to prioritize during your first hours, how the combat system works, how to approach Hero recruitment and which mistakes can slow down your early progression.

**Quick Answer:**
Follow the main story until the game opens its core systems, learn one reliable three-Hero combat chain, spend early resources carefully, explore nearby side areas and avoid upgrading every Hero at the same time.

**Beginner Checklist (13 items):**
1. Complete the opening tutorials
2. Learn the dodge and camera controls
3. Understand Active Skills
4. Identify which Status Ailments your Heroes apply
5. Learn how Signal Skills appear
6. Build one functional three-Hero team
7. Follow the main story until core systems unlock
8. Check new Hero and recruitment menus
9. Save limited upgrade materials
10. Explore nearby caves and watchtower cellars
11. Collect ingredients and test Cooking
12. Review Familiar functions
13. Install and run the game from an SSD

**What To Do First:**
1. Complete the Opening Tutorials - basic attacks, Active Skills, dodging, camera control, Hero switching, status indicators and Signal prompts
2. Follow the Main Story Until Core Systems Open - main quest → unlock new system → test → explore → return to story
3. Learn One Reliable Combat Chain - Starter applies condition → second Hero activates Signal Skill → third Hero continues/finishes

**Combat System:**
- Status Ailments: Every one of the 19 playable Heroes has distinct Status Ailment abilities
- Signal Skills: follow-up attacks available when required combat condition is met
- Switching Signals: let Heroes continue entering and leaving combat during a combo

**First Team Building:**
Three roles: Starter (creates first condition), Connector (responds and creates next opening), Finisher (converts opening into damage/control). These are team positions, not permanent classes.

**Lute Early:**
Lute is the central protagonist, natural starting point for learning movement, dodging, basic Active Skills and switching. But not automatically best permanent choice for every team/boss.

**Hero Unlocks:**
19 playable Heroes, unlocked through normal progression, not randomized paid character pulls. Check roster after major story quests, review recruitment menus, save recruitment currency.

**Resource Strategy:**
Concentration rather than equal investment. Develop one Starter, one Connector, one Finisher. Upgrade high-use skills first. Keep reserve of rare materials.

**Exploration Tips:**
Activate nearby travel points. Check buildings, watchtowers and caves. Collect ingredients and speak to residents. Mark locations you cannot complete yet.

**Familiars & Cooking:**
Familiars are collectible companions supporting exploration and travel (separate from combat Heroes). Cooking combines ingredients into dishes with temporary food effects.

**Common Beginner Mistakes:**
- Upgrading every Hero too early spreads materials thin
- Ignoring Status Ailments makes Signal Skills appear less often
- Treating switching as cooldown reset breaks combo structure
- Copying Tier List without understanding team order
- Spending recruitment currency too early
- Ignoring side exploration

**Boss Tips:**
Check if boss is resisting a condition, if setup skill is missing, if long animations are being interrupted. Prioritize survival while learning.

**PC Setup:**
Install on SSD, update graphics driver, restart PC, close background apps, check frame-rate limit.

**角色解锁方式：**
- 通关故事章节获得tickets，在exchange中选择解锁角色
- Hero quest line（特定角色专属任务）也可获得tickets和exclusive karma
- Tower of Trials是获得exclusive karma货币的另一种方式
- 可以dismantle不需要的exclusive karma，换取currency兑换其他角色的karma

**坐骑系统（Mounts）：**
- 通过mercenary leveling和故事进度解锁
- 同一种坐骑的不同变体会解锁第二个技能，可用于其他坐骑
- 豪华版附带baby dragon，可空中任意方向冲刺
- bright crow tit（novice mercenary解锁）也可空中冲刺，需完成20个mini games解锁第二变体
- 只有一种坐骑可以下水，需早期推进故事解锁
- 水下有大量隐藏内容

**战斗系统：**
- 基础攻击、跳跃攻击、闪避攻击（dodge + attack）
- 每个角色只有2个active skills，但战斗中还有signal skill、grab skill等
- 技能冷却通过攻击敌人来减少
- 队友支援连击需要满足特定状态效果或条件（如敌人broken状态）
- 敌人白色条=stagger后容易浮空；super armor敌人很难浮空
- 切换角色有短暂无敌帧（i-frames），直到执行下一次攻击或signal skill

**推荐角色与队伍：**
- Charlotte（夏洛特）：忍者，高机动性高DPS，唯一能空中冲刺的角色，有影分身可引爆
- Johnny：高DPS，攻击后快速落地
- Raina（蕾娜）：高DPS，有sprint skill和dodge skill两种不同地面技能
- 动作型推荐队伍：Charlotte + Johnny + Raina（需操作好，可通关终局）
- 远程休闲型推荐队伍：Sice + Otellio + Dana（远程输出，通关终局更轻松）
- Lute（主角）：可以治疗，但前期不是最强，建议队伍成型后再投资

**装备系统（Gear）：**
- 每个角色有对应元素的独特装备（如Charlotte是毒属性）
- 在blacksmith制作装备，可选择main stat
- Chest piece给的main stat数值最高，其次是legs
- 装备可reforge重铸（消耗材料，RNG），可锁定想要的属性
- 套装效果只需3件触发，不需要5件
- 可混搭套装（3件+2件），如3件毒属性+2件眩晕/空中伤害

**Karma系统：**
- Karma是武器，每个角色有独特的exclusive karma
- 基础karma可在exchange shop购买，非角色专属，可通用
- 用7个相同基础karma可ascend到满级（1个基础+6个replicas）
- World bosses可掉落基础karma（RNG），每天可刷
- Exclusive karma可解锁特殊技能（如Charlotte的影分身引爆）

**Master Skill与觉醒：**
- 角色达到30级可解锁master skill（grimoire）
- 需要找到treasure maps，通常在推水晶墙谜题后的宝箱中
- 地图标记大致位置后需自行寻找（可能藏在洞穴、水下、灌木下）
- 宝箱也给awakening points，用于觉醒角色技能和解锁新招式

**货币与日常：**
- Fragment of Sacred Light：开宝箱和完成特定活动（小Boss）获得
- Regional bosses：每天早上9点刷新，奖励技能升级卷轴和锻造模具
- 完成任务获得medals，可兑换几乎所有材料和技能升级卷轴
- Moon key：开启特殊宝箱，通过探索/任务/故事获得，或用adventures emblem购买

**刷材料地点：**
- 早期即可去刷gold和角色XP
- Orbit City左下角有gear upgrade材料
- 还有专门刷karma材料的地点

**终局内容：**
- Tower of Trials（高难度）
- Raid（极端模式90级，比角色高20级）
- World bosses（每天刷新）

**新手建议：**
- 先推故事解锁核心系统，不要急着探索全图
- 集中资源培养一支主力队伍，不要平均分配
- 优先升级常用技能，保留稀有材料
- 探索时注意水下和隐藏洞穴
- 不要用奇怪的队伍去打终局内容，需要针对性配装和队伍
